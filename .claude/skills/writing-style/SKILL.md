# Writing style

Explain plainly. The prose in this repo exists to transfer understanding, not
to impress. Target register: docs/RL_DESIGN.md.

- **No rhetorical flourishes or showy metaphors.** Not "this study puts that
  decision on a dial", not "reliability never printed on the box", not
  "earning its keep". State the fact the metaphor was standing in for.
- **Don't address the reader.** No "you"; write in the third person about the
  thing itself.
- **No process narration.** Not how the result was found, iterated on, or
  reviewed — only what the thing is, how it works, and what the numbers show.
- **Concrete over vivid.** A number, a mechanism, or a condition beats an
  evocative phrase. If a sentence would fit in a pitch deck, rewrite it.
- Headings and table entries: short and descriptive, no puns or teasers.

A question as a title or section opener is fine when it *is* the research
question ("when does a cheap simulator pay off?"); everything after it should
answer soberly.
